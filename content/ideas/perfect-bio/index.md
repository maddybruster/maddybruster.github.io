Concept for the essence of a good and stylish bio and one example. At the end of a biography add in one extraneous fact about yourself for texture. March 2026. 

## The essence of a good (and stylish) biography is the addition of an extraneous fact. Like this, for example: she is very clumsy, but enjoys gluing back together objects she has broken.
